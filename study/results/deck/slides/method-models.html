Method · 1 of 3

Two models, five configurations, one fixed training recipe

Data. VisDrone2019-DET [1]: train 6,471 · val 548 · test-dev 1,610. Results on test-dev only, never tuned on it.

Models. YOLO11s [12] and YOLO26s [13], fine-tuned from COCO: 100 epochs, SGD, seed 0.

Input resolutions. 640 to 1280; compressed configurations at 640, 960 and 1120.

Accuracy. COCO-style AP and AP-small [2] at the 0.001 evaluation threshold.

ConfigurationWhat is done
baselineFine-tuned, no compression; the runtime’s default precision
quantizedPost-training INT8, per-channel weights, calibrated on val [14, 15]
pruned30 % of channels removed per layer, then recovery fine-tuning [16, 17]
distilledTrained with a YOLO11m or YOLO26m teacher [18]
combinedPruned, recovered with the teacher, then INT8

GLOSSARY.md · INVARIANTS.md sections 3, 7, 8

Split discipline: train on train, pick checkpoints on val, report test-dev, never tune on test-dev (INVARIANTS.md section 3). INT8 calibration uses all 548 val images. The pruning ratio is the fraction of channels removed, not of parameters: 30 % of channels removes about 40 % of YOLO11s’s parameters and 32 % of YOLO26s’s, so do not say "30 % smaller". Input resolutions 800 and 1120 are the 960 and 1280 checkpoints run at a resolution they were not trained at (20 cells; hollow markers in the figures). Per-channel INT8 is a fix made on 31 October after the stock recipe cost YOLO26s up to 20 % of its AP (#41); the stock cells are kept and shown separately. Metric caveat: faster-coco-eval, maxDets 500, not the official VisDrone toolkit. Absolute AP of about 0.19 at 640 is normal for VisDrone at this model scale.
