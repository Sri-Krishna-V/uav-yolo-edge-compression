The problem

Two thirds of the objects a drone sees are small

VisDrone test-dev [1]: 1,610 aerial images, 75,102 labelled objects, a median of 36 per image.

“Small” is COCO’s bucket [2]: under 32 × 32 pixels in the original image.

Small objects are the hardest case for detectors [3].

s01_objects · runs/gt/visdrone-test-640.json, COCO area buckets

Start with the data, not the models. A drone flying at altitude sees mostly tiny objects: in VisDrone’s test-dev split, 50,837 of the 75,102 labelled objects, 67.7 %, are COCO-small, under 32 by 32 pixels. Only 3.2 % are large. Images are dense: a median of 36 objects, up to 461. So any detector we put on a drone lives or dies on AP-small, and that is the metric this study reports throughout, beside AP. Source: the ground truth our evaluator builds for test-dev (runs/gt/visdrone-test-640.json), areas in original image pixels.
