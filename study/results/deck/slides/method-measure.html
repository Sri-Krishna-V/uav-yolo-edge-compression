Latency = pre-processing + runtime call + post-processing, the median over all 1,610 images, at the 0.25 deployment threshold, with its p95, as single-stream benchmarks report it [19]. Measured on the platform, never estimated from FLOPs [7].

Each session runs its cells in shuffled order, with a drift check at its start and end.

INVARIANTS.md sections 4.5 and 4.7 · cells.csv: 88 scored cells

Two thresholds: accuracy is scored at the evaluation threshold 0.001 so that every box counts; latency is measured at the deployment threshold 0.25, because at 0.001 NMS handles up to 30,000 candidates and would look far more costly than on a real drone (INVARIANTS.md 4.5). The Pi 5 is held at 2000 MHz because at the stock 2400 MHz the pilot hit the soft temperature limit; the lock costs about 9 % of latency (INVARIANTS.md 4.7). Cell counts from cells.csv by platform: 74 and 14. The Pi 4B and the Jetson were cut from the study on 1 November. OpenVINO has no INT8 cell on the Pi 5: its ARM plugin compiles the INT8 model with no int8 layer (#38). The phone is a Samsung Galaxy M34, a consumer-grade phone; the laptop is a consumer-grade laptop.
