Where should the compression budget go?

Equal-latency evaluation of compressed YOLO11s and YOLO26s detectors for small aerial objects, on a Raspberry Pi 5 and a consumer-grade phone

[Presenter names] · Supervisor: [name]

VisDrone2019-DET test-dev · 1,610 images

One sentence of motivation: drones carry CPU-class computers, small objects need pixels, and pixels cost time. The answer in one line: it depends on the configuration. INT8 is worth spending on a larger input resolution; pruning is not. Every latency in this talk is measured on the platform, never estimated from FLOPs. The Raspberry Pi 5 is the main platform. The Galaxy M34, a consumer-grade phone, is included, but its latencies are provisional because its drift checks failed.
