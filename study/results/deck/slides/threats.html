Say what each threat does to the headline. Throttling: 24 of 74 Pi 5 cells were flagged throttled, all FP32 (cells.csv, throttled column); no INT8 or NCNN cell ran an image below 2000 MHz. A throttled latency is if anything high, so it makes INT8’s speed-up look larger, and it makes two INT8 pairs look farther from their baselines than an unthrottled baseline would. A1 at 640 (389.8 ms) is within 0.3 % of the pilot’s unthrottled 388.5 ms. Session 6: its INT8 cells ran 6.2 to 7.1 % slower than session 7’s at 1120; accuracy is unaffected. Seeds: every cell is seed 0. Only YOLO11s has a measured three-seed spread at 640: 0.0007 AP and 0.0027 AP-small (#9). The two smallest Result A gains (+0.0044 and +0.0049) are about 1.6 to 1.8× that AP-small spread: a yardstick, not a test. The paired bootstrap (#48) will measure test-set sampling only, not seed variance. The phone is a consumer-grade phone with no way to lock its clock.
