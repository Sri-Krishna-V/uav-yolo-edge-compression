On the phone INT8 is about twice as fast, so INT8 at 960 lands near FP32 at 640: a 1.5× step in input resolution where the Pi 5’s INT8 buys a 1.25× step. Read the larger gain as "a bigger step was affordable", not as "the phone is better at INT8"; that is an interpretation, not a tested claim. +0.0355 is 39 % of the baseline’s AP-small. Both cells of the pair are from phone session 1, so no between-session drift enters the pair itself. The drift check (reference runs at the start and end, limit ±3 %) read +4.9 % in session 1 and +3.4 % in session 2; both were flagged and kept. Phone sessions 3 and 4 will add pairs.
