Claim: the effect exists, in the expected direction, but it is modest, so the paper does not rest on it. Ratios from tables/small_object.csv: quantized per-channel YOLO11s 9.5 % AP and 13.6 % AP-small (1.43×), YOLO26s 7.6 % and 11.0 % (1.44×); stock 10.3 / 15.0 (1.45×) and 20.3 / 29.9 (1.47×); pruned 16.1 / 18.3 (1.13×, absent) and 18.5 / 24.2 (1.31×, weak); combined per-channel 1.31× and 1.37×. The bar was set in advance: below 1.3× absent, 1.3 to 2× weak, 2× or more with a CI excluding 0 confirmed. Without Pi 5 intervals the rows can be placed only by their ratio, not confirmed. The Pi 5 reproduces the laptop: A1 stock INT8 drops 10.3 % AP and 15.0 % AP-small, against the laptop’s 10.2 % and 14.3 %. A combined cell mixes three steps, so its drop is not attributable to one of them.
