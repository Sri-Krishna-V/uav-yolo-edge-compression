Claim: the YOLO11 against YOLO26 accuracy story is modest, and the speed story goes against YOLO26s. Read the gap from the clean runtimes (NCNN FP16 and per-channel INT8), because the ONNX Runtime FP32 and OpenVINO pairs carry the throttle: their ratios swing from −2.9 % to +6.3 % (ONNX Runtime) and +2.8 % to +7.5 % (OpenVINO). NCNN FP16 from 640 to 1280: +1.31, +3.34, +4.04, +6.14, +8.97 %. Per-channel INT8 from 640 to 1120: +1.89, +3.39, +4.67, +5.85 %. YOLO26s has about 5 % more GFLOPs than YOLO11s, so it is not a lighter model; the latency gap stays under the FLOP gap up to 960, then reaches 9.0 % at 1280, and nothing in the records explains the growth. Seeds: YOLO11s’s three seeds spread 0.0027 AP-small (#9); YOLO26s’s spread is not measured, so the AP-small leads are a yardstick, not a test. Source: tables/result_c.csv.
