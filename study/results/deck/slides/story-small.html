This is the hypothesis we fixed before any compressed result existed. Compression should hurt AP-small disproportionately: confirmed at 2× the AP drop or more, with a confidence interval excluding 0; weak from 1.3 to 2×; absent below 1.3×. On the Pi 5, at 640, the ratio is 1.43× and 1.44× for per-channel INT8, 1.13× (YOLO11s) and 1.31× (YOLO26s) for pruning, and 1.31× and 1.37× for the combined configuration. The stock INT8 recipe gives 1.45× and 1.47×: the recipe changes the size of the loss, not its small-object share. So the effect is real, in the expected direction, and weak by our own bar; that is why the paper rests on the equal-latency comparison instead. Prior work reports similar ratios: about 1.3× for INT8 on VisDrone [21], 1.6 to 1.8× for pruning on road scenes [22] (from our reading of their tables). The laptop numbers (#9) have 95 % intervals: INT8 1.40× [1.27, 1.64], pruning 1.17× [0.99, 1.32]. The Pi 5 rows have none until #48 runs. If asked: these are relative drops. In absolute points AP-small loses less than AP (YOLO11s INT8: 0.0123 against 0.0179), because AP-small starts lower; the hypothesis was stated, and is tested, in relative terms.
