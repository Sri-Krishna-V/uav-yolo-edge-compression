Result C compares the two generations on the same platform. YOLO26s [13] is a little more accurate than YOLO11s [12] (+0.0016 to +0.0069 AP in FP32 on ONNX Runtime) and a little slower, and the gap widens with input resolution: on NCNN FP16 from +1.3 % at 640 to +9.0 % at 1280; with per-channel INT8 from +1.9 % at 640 to +5.8 % at 1120. We read the gap from NCNN FP16 and INT8 because no cell there throttled; the ONNX Runtime FP32 and OpenVINO ratios swing with the throttle (OpenVINO: 2.8, 3.2, 7.5, 6.9, 7.1 %, a plateau after 960; so “grows steadily” holds on NCNN). In absolute terms the gap is 2 to 70 ms, and +1.3 % at 640 is within single-run noise. YOLO26s has about 5 % more GFLOPs (22.8 against 21.7 at 640), so it is not a lighter model. Its AP-small leads of +0.0021 to +0.0026 are within one YOLO11s seed spread, a yardstick rather than a test. GPU papers that compare the two [24] report the same small accuracy lead.
