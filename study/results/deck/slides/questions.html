Research questions

Four questions, and one bar we set in advance

A · Main question

At equal latency, does a compressed model at a larger input resolution beat a baseline at a smaller one on AP-small?

B · NMS cost

What does NMS cost on the Pi 5 as images get denser, and is the NMS-free head worth it?

C · YOLO11s vs YOLO26s

Is the newer model more accurate, and is it faster, on a real platform?

D · INT8 and the runtime

How much speed does INT8 buy, what accuracy does it cost, and how much does the runtime matter?

Set on 15 October, before any compressed result: small-object degradation counts as confirmed only if AP-small drops at least 2× as much as AP, with a confidence interval excluding 0.

Small-object bar: issue #9 · re-centring on A and B: issue #10, 20 October

Be honest about the history. The study began with a small-object hypothesis. The bar was fixed on 15 October before any INT8 or pruned result: below 1.3× absent, 1.3 to 2× weak, 2× or more with a CI excluding 0 confirmed. The first check, on a consumer-grade laptop, came out weak for INT8 (1.40×) and absent for pruning (1.17×). So on 20 October the paper was re-centred on the equal-latency comparison (A) and NMS (B). C and D come from the same cells. Questions A to D were not pre-registered predictions; only the small-object bar was. Sources: #9 comments of 15 and 17 October; #10 decision of 20 October; #1 and #18.
