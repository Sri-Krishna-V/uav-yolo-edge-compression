Every decision is a GitHub issue: the question, the evidence, and the date it was decided.

github.com/Sri-Krishna-V/uav-yolo-edge-compression · map issue #1

The study was run as a research project from the start: a map issue (#1) with one ticket per decision. On 9 October the plan and training recipe were fixed (100 epochs, SGD, seed 0, 640 as the matrix resolution; #6). On 15 October, before any compressed result existed, we fixed the bar for the small-object hypothesis on #9: confirmed only at 2× or more with a confidence interval excluding 0. The first checks on a consumer-grade laptop came in weak for INT8 (1.40× and 1.51×) and absent for pruning (1.17×). On 20 October (#10) the main question became the equal-latency comparison, with NMS cost as result B. On the Pi 5 the stock clock throttled at 81 to 83 °C, so every run was locked at 2000 MHz (#29). On 31 October the stock INT8 recipe was found to halve YOLO26s’s accuracy; per-channel weights fixed it (#41). On 1 November the Pi 4B and the Jetson were cut and the results tickets #44 to #49 opened. Some decisions were posted to the tickets a day or two after they were made.
